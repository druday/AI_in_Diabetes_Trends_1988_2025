Exact three-field collaboration timelapse

This interactive bubble race tracks the leading exact three-field connected-component combinations across manuscript periods. Bubble position and size both encode absolute component counts, so the chart is designed to show persistence, intensification, and late emergence rather than within-period percentages alone.

Each bubble represents one exact three-field connected-component combination from the display union of the top 12 triads. Absolute counts drive the axis and bubble size; hovering reveals the full triad label, count, within-period share, and first/last observed period.
